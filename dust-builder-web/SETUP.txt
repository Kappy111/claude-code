===========================================
  DUST — put it online with Netlify
===========================================

This is the easy version. It really cleans your Gmail, and it needs:
  * NO Chrome extension
  * NO download or install
  * NO Google Client ID
  * NO login setup

It works because YOU are already signed in to Gmail in your browser, and
Dust just builds the right search and lets you press delete yourself.


-------------------------------------------
PUT IT ONLINE  (free, about 1 minute)
-------------------------------------------
1. Go to:  https://app.netlify.com/drop
2. Drag this ZIP file onto that page.
3. Netlify gives you a web address like:
      https://happy-dust-1234.netlify.app
   That is your live Dust site. Open it any time, or share it.


-------------------------------------------
USE IT
-------------------------------------------
1. Open your Netlify site.
2. Answer the survey:
      - From what dates?  (older than 1 year / 6 months / 1 month / any)
      - From what companies?  (type like:  amazon, netflix, groupon)
      - Also sweep the Promotions tab?  (ads & deals)
3. Click "Build my cleanup search".
4. Click "Open Gmail with this search" -- Gmail opens already searched.
5. In Gmail:
      - click the checkbox at the top-left to select the page,
      - click "Select all conversations that match this search",
      - click the trash can.
   Done! Hundreds of old emails gone.


-------------------------------------------
Safe to know
-------------------------------------------
* Deleted mail goes to the TRASH, which Gmail keeps 30 days -- easy to undo.
* Dust never selects your STARRED mail (it adds -is:starred for you).
* To free the storage for real, empty the Trash in Gmail when you're sure.
