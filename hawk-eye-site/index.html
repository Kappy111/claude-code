Get the real thing

Download Hawk Eye for Chrome

The extension is the version that really locks websites — a web page alone can't. Free, and everything stays on your computer.

⬇ Download the extension (.zip)
  1. Unzip the file you downloaded (put the folder on your Desktop).
  2. In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions
  3. Turn on Developer mode (switch, top-right).
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the hawk-eye-extension folder.
  5. Pin Hawk Eye, click its icon, and lock your first site. 🦅
Honest limits: Hawk Eye locks sites in this Chrome, on this computer. It can't use a real fingerprint reader or face camera — that needs a full installed app ("a thumbprint if their computer has it," like the original notebook said). The PIN, phrase, and pattern locks are 100% real.
