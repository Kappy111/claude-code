=================================================
  DUST — the Chrome extension
=================================================

This does the most of any version:
  * EMAILS  — auto-sweeps old junk mail to Trash, by itself, NO key needed
              (Dust clicks inside Gmail for you, like your own hands).
  * DOWNLOADS — really deletes old files Chrome downloaded, to free space.
  * PHOTOS  — opens Google's own cleaner for junk photos/videos/files.


-------------------------------------------------
BIG HEADS-UP, READ FIRST
-------------------------------------------------
To add an extension that isn't from the Chrome Web Store, you turn on
"Developer mode". On a SCHOOL or FAMILY-LINK Chromebook, the grown-up who
manages it has often TURNED THIS OFF. If it's off, the extension can't be
added -- that's the Chromebook's rule, not Dust. You'll find out at Step 2.
If it won't let you, use the no-install Dust website instead.


-------------------------------------------------
HOW TO ADD IT  (about 1 minute)
-------------------------------------------------
1. Unzip this folder somewhere you can find it (like Downloads).

2. Open Chrome and go to:   chrome://extensions
   Turn ON the "Developer mode" switch (top-right corner).
   (If the switch is missing or greyed out, your Chromebook blocks this --
    stop here and use the Dust website instead.)

3. Click "Load unpacked".

4. Pick the unzipped "dust-extension" folder (the one with manifest.json).

5. Dust appears! Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar and pin
   Dust so you can see it.


-------------------------------------------------
HOW TO USE IT
-------------------------------------------------
Click the Dust icon. Three tabs:

  EMAILS:  Tap a Quick Sweep (or build your own), then watch Gmail --
           Dust searches, selects everything that matches, and deletes it
           to Trash. Keep the Gmail tab open while it works.

  DOWNLOADS: Pick an age -> Find old downloads -> look at the list ->
             Delete. (This one is permanent, so check the list first.)

  PHOTOS:  Opens Google's storage cleaner for photos, videos and big files.


-------------------------------------------------
SAFE TO KNOW
-------------------------------------------------
* Emails go to Gmail TRASH (kept 30 days) and Starred mail is never touched.
* The email sweeper works by clicking inside Gmail for you, so it needs NO
  Google key. If Gmail ever changes its buttons it might stop part-way --
  then just finish the last click yourself, or tell Claude to fix Dust.
* Downloads delete is permanent -- review the list before deleting.
* Dust never sends your emails or files to anyone. It all stays in your
  browser and your Google account.
