Welcome to Voice Typer

Dictate anywhere in Chrome. Press your shortcut, speak, press again — the text is transcribed on your device and typed at your cursor. Nothing is sent to the cloud.

  1. Enable your microphone

    Click the button and choose Allow when Chrome asks.

  2. Your shortcut

    Default is Ctrl+Shift+Space. Change it any time here:

  3. Try it

    Click into any text box on a web page, press your shortcut, say a sentence, then press it again. The first time, the speech model downloads once (~145 MB) and then works offline.

You can close this tab when you're done. Open the 🎙️ toolbar icon any time for settings.
