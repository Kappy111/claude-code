===========================================
  DUST — put it on the web with Netlify
===========================================

This is the website version of Dust. No Python, no terminal.
Two short jobs: (1) put the site online, (2) get a Google Client ID.

Everything runs in YOUR browser. Your emails never go to anyone but Google.


-------------------------------------------
PART 1 — Put Dust online (free, ~2 minutes)
-------------------------------------------
1. Go to:  https://app.netlify.com/drop
2. Drag the ZIP file (or this whole folder) onto that page.
3. Netlify gives you a web address like:
      https://sparkly-dust-1234.netlify.app
   That's your live Dust site! Copy that address — you need it in Part 2.


-------------------------------------------
PART 2 — Get your Google Client ID (adult helps)
-------------------------------------------
Dust needs Google's permission to tidy your mailbox. Google gives you a
"Client ID" for that. It is FREE — no credit card, no billing.

NOTE: a kid's Google account can't make one, so an ADULT signs in with
their Google account for these steps. You still clean YOUR mail, because
YOU log in at the very end.

1. Go to:  https://console.cloud.google.com/
2. Top bar -> project menu -> New Project -> name it "Dust" -> Create.
   Make sure "Dust" is the selected project.
3. Search bar -> "Gmail API" -> open it -> Enable.
4. Left menu -> APIs & Services -> OAuth consent screen:
      - Choose External -> Create
      - App name: Dust, and fill in an email -> Save and continue
      - Test users -> Add users -> add YOUR Gmail address -> Save
5. Left menu -> Credentials -> Create Credentials -> OAuth client ID:
      - Application type: Web application
      - Under "Authorized JavaScript origins" click ADD URI and paste your
        Netlify address from Part 1 (for example:
        https://sparkly-dust-1234.netlify.app ) -- no slash at the end.
      - Create.
6. A box pops up with your Client ID. It ends in
   .apps.googleusercontent.com  -- copy it.


-------------------------------------------
PART 3 — Use Dust!
-------------------------------------------
1. Open your Netlify address in a browser.
2. Paste the Client ID and click "Save & connect Gmail".
3. Click "Connect my Gmail", log in as YOURSELF, click Allow.
   (You may see a "Google hasn't verified this app" screen because it's
    your own private app -> click Advanced -> Go to Dust. That's fine; it's
    your app.)
4. Answer the survey (dates, senders, promotions) and sweep!


-------------------------------------------
Safe to know
-------------------------------------------
* Dust moves mail to the TRASH, which Gmail keeps 30 days. Undo any time.
* Dust NEVER touches Starred mail.
* To actually free the space, empty the Trash in Gmail when you're sure.
* Your Client ID is not a password. It just names your app to Google.
